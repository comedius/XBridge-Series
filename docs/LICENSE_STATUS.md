# License and redistribution status

**Current repository status: UNLICENSED.** The root `LICENSE` file reserves all rights and grants no general permission to copy, modify, distribute, sublicense, or use this repository.

The repository being publicly readable on GitHub does not make it open source and does not grant reuse rights. Public visibility and copyright licensing are separate matters.

## Current rules

- Do not replace `UNLICENSED` with an open-source license by assumption.
- Do not describe XBridge as open source until the project owner has selected and applied an appropriate license.
- Do not accept or merge third-party contributions until contribution rights and licensing terms have been reviewed.
- Do not publish binaries or package third-party material until the applicable rights have been checked.
- Preserve attribution and license notices for any third-party material whose use is approved.
- Keep proprietary Xbox firmware, keys, game binaries and protected assets out of the repository.

## Owner decision still required

The project owner must choose whether the repository should remain all-rights-reserved or adopt a specific license, and obtain qualified legal advice where appropriate. This document records the current state; it does not make that decision.

## Repository publication policy

Project-related development documentation, AI-agent configuration, prompts, workflows, research and automation may be published in this public repository. This does not mean those materials are automatically part of a future end-user product distribution. Product packaging must use a separately reviewed allowlist. Private personal data and actual credentials/secrets must not be published.
